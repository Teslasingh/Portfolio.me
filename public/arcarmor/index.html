Revolutionary Concept

ARCARMOR™ turns a simple suit into an electrified deterrent shield. With embedded high-voltage electronics and conductive fabrics, any contact from assailants triggers a safe but powerful non-lethal shock, instantly repelling threats.

It's the only system combining full-body coverage, hands-free activation, and water-tolerant mobility. The integration of full-body conductive clothing with embedded non-lethal power generation for personal defense is an entirely new concept.

How It Works

01

High-Voltage Shoes

Power generator embedded in soles delivers 13,000V open circuit (low current, non-lethal) powered by lithium-ion cells.

02

Conductive Suit

Flexible, silver/aluminum threaded fabric with interlinked modules for gloves, hoodie, pants, and socks.

03

Operation

Outer layer is electrified when armed; wearer stays safe due to equipotential design. No risk to the user.

04

Shock Delivery

All attackers near or coming in contact with user get the shock, just like electric fencing
